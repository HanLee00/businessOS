# Customer Support Workflow

1. Resolve the business mailbox and business context.
2. Read the relevant conversation and authoritative order/invoice record.
3. Draft a response with links or identifiers minimized.
4. Always show the owner the exact message text and get explicit approval before sending — every time, no exception. A prior general instruction to "update the customer" or "email her" is not approval of the specific draft; the literal text must be confirmed first.
5. Record operational follow-up in the owning system, not Business OS memory.

Do not expose one business's customers, orders, pricing, or mailbox content to staff or workflows from the other business.
