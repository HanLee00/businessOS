# Monthly Close Workflow

Run separately for Gaia and Oh! Venus before producing the combined view.

1. Confirm the closing period and source completeness.
2. Reconcile sales, refunds/credits, fees, receivables, payments, expenses, and cash/bank balances.
3. Review uncategorized and duplicate entries.
4. Apply documented shared-expense allocations.
5. Produce draft P&L and exception list per business.
6. Obtain approval for adjustments.
7. Finalize separate reports, then refresh the combined owner dashboard.

Never use a combined dashboard to backfill or overwrite either ledger.
