# P&L Specification

Produce three views:

1. Gaia Gifts Co P&L
2. Oh! Venus P&L
3. Combined owner-level management view

## Minimum sections

| Section | Examples |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Product/service sales, delivery/shipping income, approved other operating revenue |
| Contra revenue | Discounts, refunds, returns, credits |
| Cost of goods sold | Products/materials, packaging, production, fulfillment, attributable courier |
| Gross profit | Net revenue minus COGS |
| Operating expenses | Advertising, software, professional fees, payroll/contractors, bank/payment fees, rent, other approved categories |
| Net profit | Gross profit minus operating expenses and approved non-operating items |

## Control rules

- Use each business's ledger or reconciled source reports.
- Use MYR as the current reporting currency unless a documented policy changes it.
- Preserve period and data-freshness metadata.
- Keep eliminations and shared-expense allocations explicit.
- Do not sum unreconciled figures or treat the dashboard as an accounting ledger.
