# Combined Owner Dashboard

The combined dashboard is a read-only management layer after separate business reports reconcile.

| Metric | Gaia | Oh! Venus | Combined |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | RM | RM | RM |
| Cost of goods | RM | RM | RM |
| Gross profit | RM | RM | RM |
| Operating expenses | RM | RM | RM |
| Net profit | RM | RM | RM |
| Cash balance | RM | RM | RM |
| Receivables | RM | RM | RM |

Each refresh records source period, source systems, fetched-at time, reconciliation status, and allocation-rule version. Combined values are calculated only when both business columns use compatible periods and definitions.
